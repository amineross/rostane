Showcase

Turn a jailbroken cellular iPhone or iPad into a wireless CarPlay receiver.

Compatibility

  • iOS 12 or newer.
  • Rootful package uses iphoneos-arm. Rootless package uses iphoneos-arm64.
  • Requires Personal Hotspot support through an active SIM or carrier provisioning.

Notes

Showcase takes over classic Bluetooth while active and restores Apple's bluetoothd when stopped. Diagnostics and packet dumps are off by default. Enable them from the info button only when collecting a report.

Beta 3 prepares the Apple authentication certificate before Personal Hotspot starts. Open Showcase while the receiver still has internet access and wait for the Start button.

Tap with three fingers during CarPlay to pause input and reveal the stop and info controls. Tap with three fingers again to resume input.

  • iPhone-sized UI support for testing on smaller screens.
  • Rootful and rootless packages share the same beta 2 line.

  • Compatibility testing is ongoing across devices and jailbreak environments.

  • Initial public beta for rootful iOS 12-era cellular iPads.
  • Wireless CarPlay receiver flow with bundled BTstack, iAP2 authentication, AirPlay setup, encrypted video, and touch input.
